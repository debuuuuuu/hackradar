  (`x-ratelimit-limit-requests: 1000`, `x-ratelimit-limit-tokens: 8000`). Daily token cap not shown (**unverified**).
- gpt-oss-120b is a reasoning model: with `max_tokens: 20` the reply was empty. Use `max_tokens ≥ 1024` and

- Budget: 8,000 tokens/min is the binding limit → batches of ~12 events must stay under ~6k tokens, paced across minutes.

## Summary preview (12 live events, 2026-10-15)

- Devpost has no description in its list (title + themes only) → summaries are short and generic; Unstop has long

- Model emits non-breaking hyphens (U+2011) → normalised to `-`.

## Rate-limit lesson (2026-10-15)
- First backfill: 12 events/call with a 15 s pause → Groq returned 429 on almost every call. Groq counts the
  **reserved** `max_tokens` as well as the prompt: ~1.7k prompt + 2,048 reserved ≈ 3.7k per call, so 4 calls/min
  = ~15k > 8,000 tokens/min. Fix: `max_output_tokens: 1500`, 30 s pause (~2 calls/min ≈ 6.4k). Backfill of 310
  events ≈ 13 min; the daily cap of 60 events ≈ 2 min.
- Also lost: with results saved only at the end, a Neon idle-connection drop during the long wait wiped the whole
  run → now saved per batch with one reconnect.
