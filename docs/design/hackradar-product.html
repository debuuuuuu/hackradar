How HackRadar works

Stop checking five websites. Every morning HackRadar sends you the 3 to 5 hackathons you would actually enter, and builds a profile of everything you've done.

I read all the sites so you don't have to [^‿^]

The problem

Too many sites, too many events

Today, without HackRadar

  • Open Devpost, Unstop, Devfolio, MLH, LinkedIn…
  • Scroll past 100+ events that don't fit you
  • Find a great one, then realise it's in exam week
  • Forget the deadline

With HackRadar

  • One message every morning
  • Only 3 to 5 picks, each with a reason
  • Events during your busy dates are greyed out
  • Reminders before every deadline
Every morning, about 5 AM IST

What happens while you sleep

  1. 1
    Collect

    HackRadar reads every hackathon from Devpost, Unstop, Devfolio and MLH. Same event on two sites? It shows once.

  2. 2
    Filter by your rules

    Remove what can't work for you. No AI here, just your settings.

    city & distanceonline / offlinestudent only?registration openam I free?
  3. 3
    Pick and explain

    AI scores what's left against your interests (AI, web3, prize, team or solo) and writes one line on why each pick fits.

  4. 4
    Send to you

    Your picks arrive by email, Discord or WhatsApp. You decide what to do with them.

    LinkedIn and X

    Hackathons that are only announced in posts

    Many college and company hackathons are only posted on LinkedIn or X. HackRadar finds them without logging in anywhere.

    1. 1
      Search for public posts

      Search services (Tavily, then Exa or Serper as backup) look for public posts like "hackathon registrations open".

    2. 2
      Read the post

      A reader tool opens the public page, the same way a logged-out visitor would see it.

    3. 3
      Pull out the details

      AI finds the name, dates and registration link in the post text.

    4. 4
      Throw out the junk

      No registration link, already over, or older than about 90 days? Dropped. Everything else joins the normal filter and ranking.

    No logins, no cookies, no fake accounts. Every pick from a post links back to that post.

    Promises

    Rules HackRadar never breaks

    Real dates and prizes

    They always come from the organiser's page, never from AI. The link is always shown.

    Your data stays yours

    The AI only sees interest tags like "AI, online". Never your name, email or history.

    No sneaky scraping

    Only public pages and official APIs. No logging into anyone's account.

    Leave anytime

    One click in any email unsubscribes you and deletes your account.
